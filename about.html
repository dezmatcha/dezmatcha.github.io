about

dez matcha is a pop-up matcha business based in bergen county, nj.

my name is alanna and my love for matcha grew while i was in college at uc irvine, the land of boba. i worked as a bobarista and loved making drinks for customers and began experimenting making my own matcha lattes at the shop (they were terrible). after graduating, i had the opportunity to travel to travel to japan where i received an indepth lesson on different matcha qualities. of course i purchased lots of matcha while being there and brought multiple tins home to make my own matcha at home. after a year of trial and error, i was able to perfect my recipe and learn even more about matcha.


after trying different matchas in the area, i realized that there weren't any that i liked as much as mine. and even if i came across a good one, it wasn't as satisfying as making it myself. i learned that i loved the process and ritual of making matcha even more than drinking it.

that being said, i thought i should share my love for matcha with the community so that everyone can enjoy a dez matcha!
