about

dez matcha is a pop-up matcha business based in bergen county, nj.

dez matcha was born from my love of tea, ritual of tea ceremony, and forming community with others.


after testing two years of testing, a trip to japan, and a desire for community, i was able to perfect a dez matcha.


that being said, i thought i should share my love for matcha with the community so that everyone can enjoy a dez matcha!
